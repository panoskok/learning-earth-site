The Learning Earth

A Philosophical Framework for Life, Consciousness and Memory

The standard account treats Earth as a habitat. This book asks what changes if the planet is read as a system that accumulates structure, and what “learning” can rigorously mean at that scale. It is written by an engineer, and it marks the line between established science and interpretation.
