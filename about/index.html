Panagiotis Kokkorogiannis

Panagiotis Kokkorogiannis is an engineer by training. He writes about the places where systems science and the oldest contemplative traditions appear to be pointing at the same territory from opposite sides.

He describes himself as an engineer, not a scientist. On this site, what is established research and what is his own reading are kept apart and marked as such.
